Localisation Is Where the Growth Is

Translation is cheap. Localisation — pricing, payment methods, storefront rules and the cultural references in the script — is where the lift actually comes from.

By June Alcaraz / 18 October 2026 / 2 min read

Translating a 40,000-word script costs $2,000 to $6,000. Localising the same game — pricing in local currency, adding the payment methods people actually use, adjusting store rules, re-recording the UI and checking the cultural references — costs $12,000 to $30,000. The second number is where the revenue is.

A studio that translated into five languages and localised into two reported revenue per market three times higher in the localised markets, over the same period and with the same marketing spend. The translated markets converted but did not retain.

The four things players notice

A price that looks wrong for the market, which reads as a scam. A payment method they do not use, which loses the sale at checkout. A store page whose screenshots are in the original language, which reads as neglect. And a script whose references point at a culture the player is not in, which reads as a translation.

“We localised the checkout and the refund rate fell by a third. The script was fine.”Localisation producer at a mid-size studio

The cheapest wins are in the checkout. Local pricing, one local payment method and a receipt in the local language cost a few thousand dollars per market and move conversion by 10 to 25 percent in the studio's own tests.

The expensive part is the script. A localised script needs a writer who can rewrite a joke rather than translate it, and that person costs more than a translator and less than a failure. Two studios now budget a writer per market and treat the translator as a second step.

Key takeaways

  • Translation costs $2,000–$6,000; full localisation costs $12,000–$30,000 per title.
  • Localised markets produced three times the revenue per market in one studio's numbers.
  • Local pricing and one local payment method move conversion 10–25 percent on their own.
