Processors Tighten Underwriting, and Studios Are Paying for It

Reserves doubled, descriptors got stricter and two mid-size studios lost their merchant IDs in a single quarter. The cost lands on the smallest operators first.

By D. Okonkwo / 4 November 2026 / 2 min read

Three studios that renewed processing contracts this autumn were asked for a 10 percent rolling reserve, up from 5, and a 180-day hold on the first three months of volume. One was moved to a new descriptor mid-contract, which is the kind of change that produces chargebacks for a fortnight and a review at the end of it.

Underwriters are reading the same signals as everyone else: chargeback ratios above 0.9 percent, subscription billing with unclear cancellation language, and a platform payout history that cannot be reconciled to the merchant statement. None of those are new problems. What changed is that acquirers now price them instead of declining them.

What a merchant ID costs now

A high-risk MID on $60,000 a month costs between 5.5 and 8 percent all-in once the reserve, the monthly minimum and the per-transaction fee are counted. Two years ago the same volume cleared at 4 to 5.5. The difference is roughly $1,800 a month for a studio of that size, which is one performer's payout.

“The rate is not the problem. The hold is the problem. I can price a rate; I cannot pay wages with a hold.”Owner of a 14-performer studio

The studios that kept their IDs did three things: they moved cancellation to a single click and logged it, they separated trial billing from recurring billing so the descriptor matched what the customer saw, and they stopped routing affiliate traffic through the same MID as their own site. All three are unglamorous and all three show up in the chargeback ratio within a quarter.

The alternative is a payment aggregator, which shifts the underwriting to a third party and charges 1 to 2 points more for the privilege. Two studios took that route this year and describe it as renting a merchant account rather than owning one.

Key takeaways

  • Reserves have moved from 5 to 10 percent with 180-day holds on new volume.
  • All-in cost for a high-risk MID on $60,000 a month now runs 5.5–8 percent.
  • Clear cancellation, separated trial billing and separate affiliate MIDs are what keep a merchant ID alive.
