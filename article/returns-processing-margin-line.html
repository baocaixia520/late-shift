Returns Are the Margin Line Nobody Forecasts

A 14 percent return rate on a 42 percent margin product leaves 3 points of profit. Most brands find this out in January, after the guide has been printed.

By June Alcaraz / 6 October 2026 / 2 min read

Returns on novelty products run 9 to 18 percent depending on category and channel, and the cost of processing one is $6 to $11 once shipping, inspection, repackaging and the occasional write-off are counted. A product with a 42 percent gross margin and a 14 percent return rate is left with roughly 3 points of profit before overhead.

The number is rarely in the plan. Brands forecast sell-through, not return rate, and the return rate only appears when the first January statement lands. Two distributors have started to print an assumed return rate on the internal product sheet, next to the margin, so that the buyer sees both numbers.

What reduces the rate

Not better product photography, which is the usual answer. The three interventions that showed measurable movement were an accurate size or specification table, a single clear returns page with a prepaid label, and a packaging insert that told the buyer what to expect. The last one is cheap and reduced returns by 2 points at one brand.

“We were losing margin on people who were surprised, not on people who were unhappy.”Ecommerce lead at a novelty brand

The channel matters more than the product. Marketplace returns run higher than direct-to-consumer because the buyer often does not choose the seller, and a marketplace listing that inherits another seller's images inherits their return rate too. One brand moved its entire catalogue off a marketplace and cut returns by 5 points in a quarter.

The accounting matters as well. A returned unit that can be resold costs the processing fee and nothing else. One that cannot is a full write-off plus the fee, which is why inspection at the dock, rather than at the warehouse, is the cheapest place to decide.

Key takeaways

  • Returns run 9–18 percent and cost $6–$11 per unit to process.
  • A 14 percent return rate on a 42 percent margin product leaves about 3 points of profit.
  • Specification accuracy and a prepaid label move the rate more than better photography.
