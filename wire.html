Announcements submitted by companies and published as received, with the source named. Wire items are not reported or edited by the desk beyond length and house style.

  • Release14 November 2026

    Kestrel Adds Same-Day Settlement for Cam Studios and Membership Sites

    The processor says merchants clearing between $150,000 and $900,000 a month can now opt into same-day settlement for a 0.35 percent surcharge. The service is live in six markets and requires a twelve-month processing commitment.

    Source: Kestrel Payments
  • Release12 November 2026

    Northline Talent Opens Second Office in Lisbon

    The agency has hired four staff for a Lisbon office covering Portuguese and Spanish-speaking performers. Managing partner Ilse Bergström said the office will handle contract administration for the European roster rather than new signings in its first year.

    Source: Northline Talent
  • Release11 November 2026

    Harbor & Vine Publishes Fourth-Quarter Novelty Assortment Guide

    The distributor's guide covers 62 products across four categories, with reorder cut-offs listed alongside each listing. The company said featured items represent about 60 percent of expected fourth-quarter revenue.

    Source: Harbor & Vine
  • Release9 November 2026

    Sunder Studio Network Releases Compliance Template Library

    The cam studio network has published twelve document templates covering age assurance records, reserve reconciliation and performer agreement assignment. Templates are free to member studios and licensed to non-members at $400 for the set.

    Source: Sunder Studio Network
  • Release6 November 2026

    Pallas Interactive Ships Chapter Four of Longwater

    The text-first adult narrative title adds roughly 90 minutes of play and a new progression track. The studio confirmed a five-to-six week cadence through the first quarter and said localisation into German and Spanish is in review.

    Source: Pallas Interactive
  • Release4 November 2026

    Meridian Verification Opens Membership Waitlist

    The booking-industry clearinghouse will accept up to twelve agencies in its first open cohort. Membership includes the shared standard, the appeal reviewer and per-check pricing at $18.

    Source: Meridian Verification
  • Release30 October 2026

    Olive Row Retail Partners With Rivet Games on Holiday Bundle

    The bundle pairs a novelty product line with a game code and will be sold through the retailer's direct store from 20 November. Neither company disclosed terms; the retailer said the run is limited to 4,000 units.

    Source: Olive Row Retail
  • Release28 October 2026

    Bellweather Confirms Winter Trade Floor Dates

    The winter floor runs 2 to 4 December in Amsterdam, with 78 confirmed exhibitors across studios, novelty retail and payment services. Exhibitor bookings for the following season open on the closing day.

    Source: Bellweather Events
