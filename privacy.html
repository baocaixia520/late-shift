Privacy

A short policy, because the site does very little.

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

This site is a static publication. It sets no advertising cookies, loads no third-party scripts and runs no analytics. The only thing stored in your browser is nothing at all unless you submit a form, in which case the form's behaviour is described below.

What the hosting provider sees

Like any website, the server that delivers these pages receives your IP address, the page requested, the time and your browser's user agent. Those logs are held by the hosting provider for a short retention period for security and abuse purposes and are not combined with any other data by us.

Forms

The newsletter and contact forms in this build are demonstrations. They validate an address in the browser and do not transmit it anywhere until you connect them to an email provider. Once connected, the provider's own privacy terms apply and the address is used only to send the briefing.

Email

If you write to the editorial desk, your message and address are kept for as long as needed to answer it and to correct the record if the message concerns a published story.

Your rights

Write to augustgrant33@outlook.com to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. We answer within 30 days.
