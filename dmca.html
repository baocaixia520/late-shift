Content policy and takedowns

What we host, what we do not, and how a rights complaint is handled.

This publication writes about companies. It does not host adult content, embed third-party video, or publish performer imagery. Our own photography is licensed or original.

Our images

Every photograph on this site is either commissioned, licensed or generated for editorial use. None carries a third-party watermark, and none is taken from another publication. If you believe an image infringes your rights, tell us and we will remove it while we check.

Takedown requests

Send a notice to augustgrant33@outlook.com with:

  • The URL of the material you object to.
  • Identification of the work you say is infringed, and the basis of your claim.
  • Your name, address and a contact route.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorised, and that the information in the notice is accurate.

We acknowledge within two business days and act within seven, either by removing the material or by explaining why we believe the use is permitted. If a story is affected, we publish a note rather than quietly deleting it.

Corrections and right of reply

Companies that believe a story misstates them may submit a reply. We publish replies that add information, and we correct errors with a dated note. We do not remove accurate reporting on request.
