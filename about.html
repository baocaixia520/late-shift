About Late Shift

Who we are, what we cover, and how the desk is funded.

Late Shift is an independent trade publication covering the business of the adult industry. We write for the people who run studios, book talent, ship product, underwrite merchant accounts and read the statute before it takes effect.

What we cover

  • Talent & Agency News — Signings, agency deals and the money side of representation. Who signs whom, at what percentage, and what the contract says when it ends.
  • Adult Business News — Platform economics, consolidation, processing, payouts and valuations. Reported from term sheets, merchant statements and the people who sign them.
  • Novelty & Retail News — Product launches, assortment, packaging, distribution and the stores that carry the margin after the holiday rush.
  • Legal & Policy News — Age verification, reporting duties, IP disputes, debanking and the statutes that quietly reset operating budgets.
  • Tech for Adult Webmasters — Hosting, delivery, moderation, search and the infrastructure behind membership and cam sites. Written for the people who carry the pager.
  • Games for Adult Audiences — Adult game releases, FMV and visual novels, storefront policy and the retention economics behind the launch.
  • EU News — Brussels, member-state regulators and how European rules reach operators who have never set foot in the single market.

How we work

Stories are reported from primary documents where they exist: processor statements, term sheets, committee drafts, inspection reports and the internal memos operators share when they are angry enough. Where a source will not go on the record, we say so in the copy rather than in a footnote.

We do not accept payment for coverage. Sponsored material appears only in the Wire section and is labelled as a release, with the submitting company named. The Briefing newsletter carries no sponsored slots inside the copy.

Corrections

Corrections are published at the foot of the story with the date and the change. If you believe something is wrong, write to augustgrant33@outlook.com with the URL and the sentence in question.

This publication covers the adult entertainment industry and is intended for readers aged 18 and over. It does not link to, host or promote adult content.
