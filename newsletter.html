The Thursday Briefing

One email, one number, one calendar. Every Thursday.

One email on Thursday, before the trade floors open in Europe and while the US desk is still on its second coffee. Written by the reporters who filed the stories that week.

Free. One click to leave. Demo build: connect the form to your provider.

What is in it

  • Three stories with the detail that did not fit the article.
  • One number, explained, usually from a filing.
  • The week's calendar: floors, deadlines and filings that fall due.
  • Wire items worth your time, labelled as releases.

The archive

Every issue is published to the site two weeks after it is sent, so the archive is searchable and linkable. Subscribers get it first, which is the only real advantage of subscribing.

We do not sell the list, rent the list or share an address with a sponsor. Sponsorship runs inside the issue, never against the audience.
