Sofia Reyes

Brazilian performer (2014-2021) who later worked as a producer in São Paulo.

By Editorial desk / 12 November 2026 / Biographical profile

Sofia Reyes was born in Sao Paulo, Brazil in 1993 and is Brazilian. Active from 2014-2021, this profile records publicly available career information.

Sofia Reyes was born in Sao Paulo in 1993 and started performing in 2014, initially with local productions before signing with a regional distributor. Her work reached audiences across Latin America and, later, Europe.

During 2017 and 2018 she appeared in a number of co-productions between Brazilian and European studios, and was invited to speak on a panel about cross-border distribution at an industry conference in Lisbon.

After stepping back from performing in 2021 she trained in production management and now coordinates shoots for a São Paulo studio, with a focus on scheduling and crew logistics.
