Dana Cole

American performer (2012-2020) who moved into talent representation after retiring.

By Editorial desk / 13 November 2026 / Biographical profile

Dana Cole was born in Columbus, Ohio, USA in 1991 and is American. Active from 2012-2020, this profile records publicly available career information.

Dana Cole was born in Columbus, Ohio in 1991 and began her career in 2012 with independent productions shot in the Midwest. She built a reputation for consistent output and for collaborating with first-time directors.

From 2015 she worked under a long-term contract with a mid-size studio, appearing in a steady run of titled releases and attending two annual industry expos as a featured guest. Colleagues frequently cited her professionalism on set.

She retired from performing in 2020 and completed a business qualification the following year. She now works as an agent at a talent representation firm, focusing on contract terms and newcomer onboarding.
