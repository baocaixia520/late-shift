Submit a release

Short, attributed, sourced. Here is the bar.

Wire items are short, factual and clearly attributed. We publish most releases that meet the rules below and decline the rest without explanation. There is no charge for editorial consideration; paid distribution is a separate product described on the advertising page.

What we publish

  • Product launches, funding, appointments, partnerships, event confirmations and awards.
  • 150 to 250 words, written in the third person, with a named contact and a working link.
  • One image per release, landscape, at least 1200 pixels wide, with a caption and a credit line.

What we decline

  • Claims without a source, comparative claims about competitors, and anything that reads like a rate card.
  • Releases about performers' scenes, which are not trade news and are not covered here.
  • Anything requiring a link that resolves to adult content.
